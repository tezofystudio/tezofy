🔒 Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

What we collect

When you create a free account or sign in with Google or Facebook, we store only your name and email address, plus your login method (email / Google / Facebook). We never see or store your Google or Facebook password.

What we do with it

  • Keep your account and saved prompts linked to you.
  • Occasionally email you about new prompts, features and updates.

What we never do

  • We never sell, rent or share your data with third parties.
  • We never run ads based on your personal information.
  • Your account password (for email sign-up) never leaves your own device.

Where data lives

Basic account details are saved privately in a Google Sheet owned by the TEZOFY team. Saved prompts, likes and preferences live in your own browser storage on your device.

Your choices

Want your account removed? Open an issue on our GitHub page and we will delete your entry.
