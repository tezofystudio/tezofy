Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about using TEZOFY for AI image generation.

What is TEZOFY?
TEZOFY is a premium AI image prompt library — a curated collection of pre-tested, professionally crafted prompt templates. Browse, customize and copy prompts, then paste them into tools like Google Gemini, ChatGPT or Midjourney to generate stunning portraits.
Do I need an account?
Every prompt is free to copy without an account. Creating a free account (tap the profile icon in the header) unlocks a personal profile, daily streak tracking, and a clean home for your saved prompts and likes — all stored privately on your device in this free version.
How do the Customize options work?
Every prompt page has a Customize panel with scrollable option rows — Outfit, Background, Lighting, Time of Day, Camera Angle, Mood, Extra Effects and Aspect Ratio. Just tap the options you want; the prompt text rewrites itself live. Then press Copy once — your personalized version is on the clipboard.
Is TEZOFY free to use?
Yes! Every template in the library is completely free to copy and use as many times as you like. A future VIP collection with exclusive designs is planned, but the core library will always remain free.
Which AI tools work with TEZOFY prompts?
TEZOFY prompts are designed for all major AI image generators, including Google Gemini, ChatGPT, Microsoft Designer (Bing Image Creator), Meta AI and Midjourney. Each template page notes which tool works best for that specific prompt.
How do I use a prompt template?
Find a template you like, tap it, customize any highlighted placeholders (like [CITY] or [COLOR]), then press Copy. Paste the prompt into your favourite AI tool — with a reference photo if the template asks for one — and generate. The whole process takes under a minute.
What categories of prompts are available?
The library covers festival themes (Durga Puja, Eid, Diwali), couple portraits, cinematic scenes, retro film looks, editorial fashion and more — with professional headshots and 3D name art coming soon. New templates are added every week.
Can I use AI-generated images commercially?
Usage rights depend on the AI tool you generate with — most major platforms allow commercial use under their own terms of service. TEZOFY prompts themselves are free to use anywhere. Always check your generator's licensing terms.
How often are new prompts added?
New templates drop weekly, with special collections released around festivals, weddings and trending social-media themes. Seasonal collections appear right before each major celebration.
Does TEZOFY work on mobile?
Absolutely — the site is designed mobile-first with an app-style bottom navigation, one-tap copy, and touch-friendly cards. Add it to your home screen for an app-like experience. No install required.
