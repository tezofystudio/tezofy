Is everything here really free?＋
Yes — all live tools run on free community AI engines, fully keyless and client-side. Peak hours can take 20–60 seconds; a patient retry usually wins.
How does Daily Magic Spin pick my prompt?＋
It draws a random prompt from TEZOFY's 1,000+ hand-crafted library (with a built-in fallback set), then asks the free image engine to paint it live with a fresh random seed — so no two spins are alike.
Where does my data go?＋
Nowhere. Spins, forges and generations happen between your browser and the free AI engine; TEZOFY never sees or stores them.
